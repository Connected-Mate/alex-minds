If asked about accountability

The model executes. The operator follows. The timetable describes. Accountability sits with whoever defined what "acceptable" is — acceptable delay, error rate, customer impact. Often years before the model existed.

That someone is in this room.

If asked about the human role

Three things AI can't take :

1 · Defining what "good" means when there is no precedent.
2 · Holding accountability when things go wrong publicly.
3 · Deciding what NOT to automate — and why.

All three are about being someone humans hold to account.
